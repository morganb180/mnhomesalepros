Before you hire

Ask the city or county whether a transfer inspection applies.

Minnesota has no single statewide septic-at-sale rule. Many counties and cities require an SSTS compliance inspection when a property transfers. The inspection has to be done by an MPCA-licensed SSTS inspector business.

SSTS rules and the full list →

A test is not required to sell. Disclosure is.

Under the Minnesota Radon Awareness Act (Minn. Stat. 144.496), the seller discloses known radon results and gives the buyer the MDH publication. MDH says to start with a short-term test, which usually runs 2 to 7 days.

How MDH says to test →

MDH recommends fixing at 4 pCi/L or higher.

MDH says a mitigation system generally costs $1,500 to $3,000 and must be installed by an MDH-licensed mitigation professional. Systems installed on or after January 1, 2019 need an MDH system tag.
