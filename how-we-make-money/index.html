How we make money

MN Home Sale Pros is free to use. Here is how it is, and will be, paid for, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

Today

  • Nothing is for sale. Every listing is free and built from official public records.
  • Listings are ordered by location and name. No one can pay to appear higher, to get a license badge, or to have a competitor removed.

Planned

  • Featured placements, clearly labeled "Ad", shown alongside, never instead of, the full free list.

Our rules

  • Paid or not, every listing must match a current official record.
  • Guides and cost pages are written independently of advertisers and cite their sources.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a link, the link will say so.

Professionals: see claim or correct your listing.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public MPCA SSTS, MDH radon and Saint Paul TISH records, retrieved October 12, 2026. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing isn't an endorsement. Some county names come from ZIP codes via GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

How they're ordered

By location and name. No one can pay to be ranked higher. Any future paid placement will be labeled Ad and won't reorder the free list.

Verify before you hire

Check the credential on the listing against the MPCA SSTS list, the MDH radon provider search, or Saint Paul's approved evaluator list. Minneapolis TISH: the city's finder, 612-673-5840.

Licensed Minnesota pro? Your free listing is built from public records. Claim it to correct details. Homeowners reach you when they ask to be connected.
