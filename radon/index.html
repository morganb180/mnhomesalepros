Radon in a Minnesota home sale

  • Disclosure, not a mandatory test: under the Minnesota Radon Awareness Act (Minn. Stat. § 144.496), before signing a purchase agreement the seller must disclose known radon test results and mitigation information and give the buyer the MDH radon publication. No test is required.
  • Licensing: radon measurement and mitigation professionals and companies must be licensed by MDH. Mitigation systems installed from Sept. 1, 2020 carry an MDH system tag (MDH).
