Get free quotes from up to 3 licensed Minnesota pros

Selling or buying a home in Minnesota? Tell us what you need and where. We send your request to up to 3 licensed pros nearest you that offer that service (MPCA septic inspectors, MDH radon pros, or Saint Paul-approved TISH evaluators), and email you their names. They contact you directly. It's free, and there's no obligation.

What do you need?

Not sure? We'll start with licensed radon testers. Septic inspections and Saint Paul TISH evaluations are separate; pick them if your sale needs one.

How it works

  1. We match your ZIP to licensed pros that offer the service you chose, closest first, within their published service area. TISH requests go only to Saint Paul-approved evaluators, for Saint Paul addresses. Pros can't pay to be picked.
  2. Each pro gets your name, contact details, ZIP, what you need and your note by email.
  3. You get an email listing the pros we contacted. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. We only send you tips and offers if you tick the optional box.

Prefer to choose yourself? Browse every licensed pro by county, or see septic inspectors, radon pros and Saint Paul TISH evaluators.
