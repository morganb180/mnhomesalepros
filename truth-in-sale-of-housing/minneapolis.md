# Minneapolis Truth in Sale of Housing (TISH)

> Last updated 2026-10-13. Rules on this page are checked against the official sources listed at the end of the page.

Reviewed by MN Home Sale Pros staff

**Quick answer:** Minneapolis requires a TISH evaluation by a city-licensed evaluator for most home sales. Find a licensed evaluator with the city's official Evaluator Finder, then confirm details with the Minneapolis TISH office (612-673-5840).

Minneapolis requires a TISH evaluation by a city-licensed evaluator for most home sales ([City of Minneapolis TISH](https://www.minneapolismn.gov/resident-services/property-housing/buying-selling/tish/); TISH office 612-673-5840).

Minneapolis licenses private TISH evaluators. Find one with the city's official [Evaluator Finder](https://apps.ci.minneapolis.mn.us/evaluatorfinderapp/evaluatorfinderrpt.aspx), then confirm details with the TISH office.
