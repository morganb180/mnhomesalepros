About our data

  • SSTS: MPCA public REST API licensed-businesses (3,713 business records), retrieved 2026-10-12. Kept businesses with a Current license and an inspector, intermediate inspector or advanced inspector designation: 337 kept after 5 duplicate license numbers were dropped. Expired records (2,400 of 3,713 overall) are excluded. The same data is listed on the Minnesota Geospatial Commons. Terms: public government data, with no license stated on the API. Confirm before launch.
  • Radon: MDH Radon Service Providers (Measurement, Mitigation, Analysis Laboratory; single-family service type), paged through the public search on 2026-10-12: 343 + 114 + 14 rows. Grouped into 265 businesses by company name + ZIP. Websites appear as printed by MDH and weren't fetched. No expiry dates are shown, so the list is MDH's current roster.
  • Saint Paul TISH: approved evaluator table on stpaul.gov, 37 evaluators.
  • Other cities: point-of-sale programs in nine cities, each linked to the official city page and checked 2026-10-13. Minneapolis evaluators: the city's own finder.
  • County comes from MPCA's county field, or ZIP via GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Log: data/cleanup-log.csv.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public MPCA SSTS, MDH radon and Saint Paul TISH records, retrieved October 12, 2026. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing isn't an endorsement. Some county names come from ZIP codes via GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

How they're ordered

By location and name. No one can pay to be ranked higher. Any future paid placement will be labeled Ad and won't reorder the free list.

Verify before you hire

Check the credential on the listing against the MPCA SSTS list, the MDH radon provider search, or Saint Paul's approved evaluator list. Minneapolis TISH: the city's finder, 612-673-5840.
