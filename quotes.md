# Get free quotes from up to 3 licensed Minnesota pros

Selling or buying a home in Minnesota? Tell us what you need and where. We connect you with up to 3 nearby licensed firms that offer that service (MPCA septic inspectors, MDH radon pros, or Saint Paul-approved TISH evaluators). They contact you directly. Their names appear on the confirmation screen. It's free, and there's no obligation.

Use the form at https://mnhomesalepros.com/quotes/ (needs JavaScript or a normal form post).

## How it works

1. We match your ZIP to licensed pros that offer the service you chose, closest first, within their published service area. TISH requests go only to Saint Paul-approved evaluators, for Saint Paul addresses. Pros can't pay to be picked.
2. Each firm receives your name, contact details, ZIP, what you need and your note.
3. Their names appear on the confirmation screen. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. We only send you tips and offers if you tick the optional box.
