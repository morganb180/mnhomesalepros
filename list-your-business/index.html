List, claim, correct or remove a listing

Every listing on MN Home Sale Pros comes from the MPCA, MDH or city record you are listed from. Use this form to claim your free listing (add a phone number, website or service area), fix an error, ask to be added, or ask for a listing to be removed.

This opens your email app with the request filled in, addressed to hello@mnhomesalepros.com. If nothing opens, email us directly with the same details.

What happens next

  1. We check the request against the MPCA/MDH/the city record. For claims and corrections we confirm by replying to an email address or phone number on that record, or one you can show is yours.
  2. Approved changes go live with the next site update, usually within 5 business days.
  3. Removal: we remove personal details (for example a home address or personal phone) on request. If you are no longer licensed or certified, tell us and we'll drop the listing. Because listings mirror a public record, we may keep the business name and license status while it stays on the official list.

Listings are free and ordered by location and name, never by payment.

Disclaimer: Listings come from public Minnesota records: MPCA SSTS licensed businesses (MPCA API), MDH radon service providers (MDH) and the City of Saint Paul's approved TISH evaluators (stpaul.gov), all retrieved 2026-10-12.
