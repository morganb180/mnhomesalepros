List, claim, correct or remove a listing

Every listing on MN Home Sale Pros comes from the MPCA, MDH or city record you are listed from. Use this form to claim your free listing, fix an error, ask to be added, or ask for a listing to be removed. Public pages show the credential, people's names and the mailing address. They do not show a phone number, email or website.

This opens your email app with the request filled in, addressed to hello@mnhomesalepros.com. If nothing opens, email us directly with the same details.

What happens next

  1. We check the request against the MPCA/MDH/the city record. For claims and corrections we confirm by replying to an email address or phone number on that record, or one you can show is yours.
  2. Approved changes go live with the next site update, usually within 5 business days.
  3. Removal: we remove personal details (for example a home address or personal phone) on request. If you are no longer licensed or certified, tell us and we'll drop the listing. Because listings mirror a public record, we may keep the business name and license status while it stays on the official list.

Listings are free and ordered by location and name, never by payment.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public MPCA SSTS, MDH radon and Saint Paul TISH records, retrieved October 12, 2026. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing isn't an endorsement. Some county names come from ZIP codes via GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

How they're ordered

By location and name. No one can pay to be ranked higher. Any future paid placement will be labeled Ad and won't reorder the free list.

Verify before you hire

Check the credential on the listing against the MPCA SSTS list, the MDH radon provider search, or Saint Paul's approved evaluator list. Minneapolis TISH: the city's finder, 612-673-5840.

Licensed Minnesota pro? Your free listing is built from public records. Claim it to correct details. Homeowners reach you when they ask to be connected.
