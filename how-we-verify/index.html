How we verify listings

Listings come from public Minnesota records, all retrieved and checked on 2026-10-12: MPCA SSTS licensed businesses (inspector, intermediate inspector or advanced inspector designation), MDH-licensed radon service providers, and the City of Saint Paul's approved TISH evaluators.

On this page

  1. What we check
  2. What we don't verify
  3. Corrections

What we check

  • Each business holds a current license on the agency source at retrieval; license designations are shown on the listing.
  • Businesses are filed by the county of their address of record. MDH service areas are shown as published.

What we don't verify

  • Workmanship, prices, insurance or availability.
  • We do not publish a firm's phone, email or website. A claim can correct the name, credential and address we show, after we check it against the official record.

Corrections

Spot an error? Use list, claim, correct or remove a listing. Every request will be checked against the official credential record before anything changes. See also about our data.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public MPCA SSTS, MDH radon and Saint Paul TISH records, retrieved October 12, 2026. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing isn't an endorsement. Some county names come from ZIP codes via GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

How they're ordered

By location and name. No one can pay to be ranked higher. Any future paid placement will be labeled Ad and won't reorder the free list.

Verify before you hire

Check the credential on the listing against the MPCA SSTS list, the MDH radon provider search, or Saint Paul's approved evaluator list. Minneapolis TISH: the city's finder, 612-673-5840.

Licensed Minnesota pro? Your free listing is built from public records. Claim it to correct details. Homeowners reach you when they ask to be connected.
