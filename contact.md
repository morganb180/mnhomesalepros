# Contact MN Home Sale Pros

MN Home Sale Pros is a small independent directory and guide. We read every message. We aren't a contractor, inspector or government agency, and we can't do home-sale inspections work ourselves.

## Email

[hello@mnhomesalepros.com](mailto:hello@mnhomesalepros.com): questions, data corrections and advertising. When writing about a specific listing, please include its listing ID (for example the business's license number).
