Minnesota licensed pros by county

Last updated 2026-11-01. Every number on this page is counted from our own listing records (638 listings built from MPCA, MDH and City of Saint Paul lists retrieved 2026-10-12). It counts listings, not every licensed person in Minnesota.

Reviewed by MN Home Sale Pros staff

Our records hold 638 listings: 336 MPCA-licensed septic (SSTS) businesses, 265 MDH-licensed radon businesses and 37 Saint Paul-approved Truth-in-Sale of Housing evaluators. By business address, the counties with the most listings are Hennepin County (69), Saint Louis County (24), Dakota County (22).

How we count

  • One row per listing. A listing is a licensed business, or for Saint Paul TISH a city-approved evaluator.
  • County. Comes from the business address on the licence list. A business can work in many counties, so the count shows where pros are based, not every county they serve.
  • Services. A radon business can hold more than one licence type (measurement, mitigation, laboratory), so the service columns can add up to more than the listing total.
  • Saint Paul TISH evaluators are approved citywide and have no county on the city list. They are counted separately below, not in the county table.

Frequently asked questions

How many licensed radon businesses are in Minnesota?

Our records list 265 MDH-licensed radon businesses (234 offering measurement, 96 mitigation and 14 laboratory), from the MDH service provider list retrieved 2026-10-12. 42 of them have an address outside Minnesota.

Which county has the most licensed pros?

By business address, Hennepin County has the most listings in our records (69).

Does a county with no listing mean no one serves it?

No. Counties come from the business address. Many septic and radon pros travel, so check county pages and ask pros directly.

Can I download the data?

Yes. The county table is a CSV on this page, and every listing is in api/firms.json.
