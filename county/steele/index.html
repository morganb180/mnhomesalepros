Do I need a septic inspection to sell a house in Steele County, MN?

Minnesota has no single statewide septic-at-sale rule, but many counties and cities require an SSTS compliance inspection at property transfer by local ordinance (MPCA local SSTS programs). 3 MDH-licensed radon businesses list an address in Steele County. Most radon pros list a statewide service area, so pros based elsewhere may also cover the county.

Is a radon test required to sell a home in Minnesota?

No. Under the Minnesota Radon Awareness Act (Minn. Stat. § 144.496), before signing a purchase agreement the seller must disclose known radon test results and mitigation information and give the buyer the MDH radon publication. No test is required.
