Is a radon test required to sell a home in Minnesota?

No. Under the Minnesota Radon Awareness Act (Minn. Stat. § 144.496), before signing a purchase agreement the seller must disclose known radon test results and mitigation information and give the buyer the MDH radon publication. No test is required.
