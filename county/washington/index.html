Frequently asked questions

How many licensed septic (SSTS) inspectors are in Washington County, MN?

3 MPCA-licensed SSTS inspector businesses list an address in Washington County (data last verified 2026-10-12). Many work across counties, so ask whether they cover your property.

Do I need a septic inspection to sell a house in Washington County, MN?

Minnesota has no single statewide septic-at-sale rule, but many counties and cities require an SSTS compliance inspection at property transfer by local ordinance (MPCA local SSTS programs). Ask the county or city SSTS office whether a transfer inspection applies. Inspections must be done by an MPCA-licensed SSTS inspector business.

Who does radon testing in Washington County, MN?

8 MDH-licensed radon businesses list an address in Washington County. Most radon pros list a statewide service area, so pros based elsewhere may also cover the county.

Is a radon test required to sell a home in Minnesota?

No. Under the Minnesota Radon Awareness Act (Minn. Stat. § 144.496), before signing a purchase agreement the seller must disclose known radon test results and mitigation information and give the buyer the MDH radon publication. No test is required.
